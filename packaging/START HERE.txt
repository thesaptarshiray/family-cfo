FAMILY CFO - a personal finance app for your family
====================================================

It runs on your own computer. Your figures stay in this folder and are never
uploaded anywhere. No account or subscription is needed.


SETTING IT UP (once, about 5 minutes)
-------------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents\Family CFO.
   (Don't run it from inside the zip, and don't move the folder afterwards.)

2. Install Python if you don't have it: https://www.python.org/downloads/
   On the first install screen, TICK "Add python.exe to PATH", then Install.

3. Double-click "Setup Family CFO" in this folder.
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   When it finishes, the app opens in your browser and a "Family CFO" shortcut
   appears on your desktop.

4. In the browser, choose a password. It protects the app on this computer.


EVERY DAY USE
-------------
Double-click "Family CFO" on the desktop. The app opens in your browser at
http://127.0.0.1:8000/  (a small minimised window keeps it running - leave it open).

Start on the Overview tab: add your family members, then fill in the tabs
(FDs, mutual funds, stocks, insurance, loans, monthly cash flow...).

Mutual funds: Mutual Funds tab -> "Import CAS statement" reads your CAS PDF
(from NSDL / CAMS / KFintech, saved without a password). Import a new one
each month and the funds update themselves.


OPTIONAL EXTRAS (Reminders & Settings tab)
------------------------------------------
- Ask Family CFO (the chat button, bottom right): needs a free Groq key.
  Sign up at console.groq.com -> Settings -> Data Controls -> turn on
  Zero Data Retention -> API Keys -> Create API Key -> paste it in the app.
  Tick "Privacy mode" to hide names of people and banks from the AI.
  PAN, account, folio and policy numbers are never sent.

- Email reminders 15 and 3 days before FD maturities and premium dates:
  needs a Gmail "app password" - the steps are on that tab.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Your data is the file family_finance.db in this folder. Copy it somewhere
  safe now and then (a pen drive, or your own cloud drive) - that is your backup.
- Don't share this folder with anyone once you have entered your data:
  family_finance.db and family_finance_settings.json hold your figures and keys.
- The app is a guide, not personal financial advice.
